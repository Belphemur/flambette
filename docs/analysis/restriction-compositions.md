## Findings (the one-paragraph version)

- Removal is UNION-shaped: 42.4% of the 66 pairs remove exactly the union of their singles' sets; 459 recipes across all pairs are extra removals (composition effects) and 0 are union-rule violations.
- Substitution composition: 99.6% of pair swap events trace to the singles' own substitutes (both-agree 1.1%, from-a 86.5%, from-b 11.8%, a-over-b 0.0%, b-over-a 0.2%); 0.4% are pair-specific substitutes upstream only renders under the combination.
- Dictionary: 28 of 39 from-ingredients have exactly ONE substitute across every payload — the swap table is largely fixed per ingredient, with 11 ingredients showing per-recipe (or per-combination) choice.
- Quantities: 99.8% of swaps keep the base quantity string verbatim; 0.1% are same-unit rescales (median ratio 2.000), 0.0% change units, 0.1% are fully re-authored.
