Meal-occasion hues — ADR-0043

Five new hues, deliberately not the four protein hues. On one screen a colour must mean one thing: hue-meat says "contains meat"; meal-dinner says "it's dinner". Every pair clears WCAG AA on its own surface, and each meal hue sits ≥36 ΔE from every protein hue.
